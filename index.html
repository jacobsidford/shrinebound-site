Shrinebound

A portrait roguelite RPG with turn-based card combat, made to play with one hand. Start as a Beginner, unlock jobs at shrines and build card combos with no caps.

Coming soon to Google Play

  • From Beginner to Legend

    Job Shrines return your memories and your power: Warrior, Mage, Thief, Archer and Gunner, across four job tiers.

  • Combos with no caps

    Elements react, statuses stack and engines multiply. If your build works, the numbers keep climbing.

  • Nothing to buy

    Free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account. Plays fully offline.
